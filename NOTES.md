##### **WHAT IS HTML**

##### **ANS-ITS AN HYPER TEXT MARKUP LANGUGE IT IS USED TO STRUCTURE THE WEBPAGE AND IT IS DEVELOPED BY BURNER LEE IN 1981**

##### 

##### **STRUCTURE OF HTML**

##### **1.<!DOCTYPE html>**

##### **IT IS DOUCUMENT TYPE REFERED TO CURRENT VERSION - HTML5**

##### 

##### 

##### **<HTML> TAG : ITS A ROOT TAG**

##### 

##### **<HEAD> TAG : ITS IS USED TO STORED MAIN INFORMATION ABOUT WEBPAGE**

##### 

##### **<META> TAG : IT IS USED TO STORED DETAILS INFORMATION ABOUT WEBPAGE AND USED FOR SEO (SEARCH ENGINE OPTIMIZATION)**

##### 

##### **<TITLE> TAG : ITS IS USED TO DISPALY TITLE TO THE WEBPAGE , TITLE WILL DISPLAY ON TAB** 

##### 

##### **<BODY> TAG :IT IS USED TO DISPLAY ON CONTAIN ON UI**

##### 

##### **WHAT IS TAG**

#####   **TAG IS A PREDEFINED WORD OR KEYWORD TO DISPLAY CONTAIN ON UI**

##### 

##### **TYPES OF TAG :1.PAIRED TAG**

#####               **2.UNPAIRED TAG**

##### 

##### **WHAT IS PAIRED TAG :A TAG WHICH HAVING OPENING AND CLOSING TAG IS KNOWN AS PAIRED TAG. EXAMPLE :<HTML> </HTML>**

##### 

##### **WHAT IS UNPAIRED TAG : A TAG WHICH HAVING OPENING TAG IS KNOWN AS UNPAIRED TAG. <META>, <BR> ,<IMAGE>**
